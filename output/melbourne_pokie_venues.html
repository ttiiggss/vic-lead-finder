Data source: Victorian Gambling and Casino Control Commission (VGCCC), "Current Gaming Expenditure by Venue" dataset (Semi-Annual EGM Venue Level Expenditure, Jul 2025 – Jun 2026 release), licensed under CC BY 4.0. EGM counts are the average number of operating machines reported for June 2026 and are updated by the regulator semi-annually. Venue coordinates were resolved via OpenStreetMap Nominatim geocoding against the venue name and council area, then cross-checked with a name-similarity filter to catch mismatches (e.g. a generic venue name resolving to the wrong same-named venue elsewhere in Melbourne). Rows shown greyed out with "Address unverified" or "Address not found" either failed geocoding or failed the similarity check and are intentionally left off the map rather than risk showing an incorrect location – verify these independently (e.g. via the venue's own website) before relying on them. This directory reflects licensed gaming machine venues only (hotels/clubs) and does not include Crown Casino, Keno-only, or wagering-only outlets. Compiled 2026-08-26.
